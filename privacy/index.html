Privacy Policy

Softpaw Care (iOS app, bundle com.rohitgrover.softpaw), published by Rohit Grover ("we", "us").
Last updated: 2026-11-01

Summary

Softpaw Care helps you keep a care log and reminders for your dogs and cats. We do not run our own servers for your data, we do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Information you enter

Pet profiles (name, species, breed, birthday, weight, photos), care logs, medication and vaccination notes you type, vet visit details, and reminder schedules. This stays on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud account.

iCloud / CloudKit sync

Data syncs between your devices through Apple's CloudKit private database tied to your Apple ID. It is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access the contents of your private iCloud database. You can turn off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. No reminder content is sent to us. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.

Vet-visit PDF sharing

When you export a PDF summary, it is created on your device and shared only with the recipients you choose through the iOS share sheet.

Purchases (Softpaw Plus)

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. We receive only the subscription status needed to unlock features.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to share analytics with app developers in iOS, Apple may provide us anonymised crash and usage reports. These are not linked to your identity.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. We may disclose information only if required by law.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for general household use and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a pet or record removes it from the app and, after sync, from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes local data; you can remove iCloud data via iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, or Qatar's PDPPL) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Because your data is held on your device and in your iCloud, you can exercise most of these directly; otherwise contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and material changes will be noted in the app.
